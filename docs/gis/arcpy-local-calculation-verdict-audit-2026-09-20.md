# ArcPy statistics evidence correction

The FeatureServer statistics cell's earlier pass counted rows locally. That
pass is not a remote-statistics result. This is a certification evidence
defect, not proof of a missing client or a failing Honua endpoint. The receipt
is not retained in this repository. The checklist records the cell as not
started.

After this correction the unchanged 376-cell denominator contained 166 passes,
4 failures, 136 blocked cells, 52 not started and 18 provisional exclusions.
ArcPy had 35 passes, 2 failures, 55 blocked and 2 provisional exclusions.
The 149 challenged exclusions and their 14 operation-specific resolutions were
unchanged. Later checklist totals supersede this stage. Historical reports
retain their original context.

Closing the statistics cell required a native remote aggregate receipt. A pass
from a different client lane cannot substitute.
