# Issue 5615 audit disposition

SRV-DB-003, SRV-GS-002, and SRV-GS-003 were fixed by PR #5652 (`32171426f00771f3acf9bbe195ef44e4749508ba`) and are not reimplemented here. The remaining nine members are fixed on this branch. The OKF graph was not regenerated.

PR #5652 review adjudication: the densify retention finding was corrected by reparsing one geometry at a time after preflight. The configured curve-vertex finding is corrected by threading `Limits.Geometry.MaxVerticesPerGeometry` into every GeoServices WKB conversion. The budget is clamped to that option's supported maximum of 100,000. Stored edits use the stricter of that budget and `Limits.Validation.MaxVertices`, matching `GeometryValidator`. Callers that omit a budget stay at the 50,000 default.
