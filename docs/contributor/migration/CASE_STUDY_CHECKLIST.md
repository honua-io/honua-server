# Case Study Checklist

Ensures case-study data is captured during the pilot, not reconstructed later.

---

## Pre-Pilot

- [ ] Case-study clause included in pilot agreement
- [ ] Quote approval process established with customer
- [ ] Baseline metrics captured (link to [Baseline Scorecard](PILOT_BASELINE_SCORECARD.md))
- [ ] Problem context documented (why migrating, current pain points, business drivers)

## During Pilot

- [ ] Weekly progress notes maintained
- [ ] Milestone screenshots captured
- [ ] Friction points and workarounds documented
- [ ] Customer feedback collected at key milestones

## Post-Pilot (Within 7 Days of Closeout)

- [ ] Endline metrics captured (link to [Endline Scorecard](PILOT_ENDLINE_SCORECARD.md))
- [ ] Delta calculation complete (baseline vs endline)
- [ ] Customer quote collected
- [ ] Draft case study written:
  - [ ] Problem statement
  - [ ] Approach taken
  - [ ] Outcomes and metrics
  - [ ] Customer quote
- [ ] Internal review completed
- [ ] Customer approval obtained

## Publication

- [ ] Named or anonymized case study finalized
- [ ] Published to proof materials
- [ ] Linked from relevant sales and marketing collateral
